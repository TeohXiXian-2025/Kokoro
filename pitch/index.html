01 · Why no one saw it

Three walls keep Mizuki's SOS invisible

🔒

The disclosure barrier

Saying "I'm struggling" to a parent or teacher feels like failing. Fear of 世間体 (what others think) keeps it inside.

Mon Skipped lunch. "I'm just not hungry."
🪜

The missing first step

Care starts at therapy. There's no gentle bridge from "something feels off" to "I need help."

Wed Stopped reading. Nobody to tell, nothing she'd download.
🌫️

Organizational blindness

Counselors visit maybe once a week. Teachers already work 56 h/week, the longest in the OECD. Schools notice only when attendance collapses.

Fri Late 3 days in a row. Marked "late". Nothing else.
Everyone saw a piece. No one saw the pattern.
